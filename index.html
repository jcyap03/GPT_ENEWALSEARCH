Works with either the xD/xC style export, or a BSS INFOVIEW style export — this tool auto-detects which one you give it.

Format 1 — xD/xC style:
A sheet named xD with: CMPNY_NM, CUG_NM, ACCT_NUM, SUBS_MSISDN, SUBS_PRMRY_OFRNG, category, EST_EXPIRY_DATE, commitment, occ, net_cv.
A sheet named xC with MSISDN and STATUS is optional, for the "Active only" filter.

Format 2 — INFOVIEW style:
Any sheet with: BILLING_ACCOUNT_NUMBER, MSISDN, PRODUCT_NAME, STATUS, CONTRACT_END_DATE, COMMITMENT_FEE, COMPANY_NAME, BSS_ID. Status and source (CELCOM/DIGI) are picked up automatically here — no separate tab needed.

Worked out for you automatically: Remaining Months, Penalty Fee (monthly fee × remaining months + RM100 admin), and Renewal Type (P1 ≤2 months, P2 ≤6, P3 ≤9, P4 beyond, or No Contract if expired).
